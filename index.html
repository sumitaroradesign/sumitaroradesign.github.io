Design philosophy

A few principles that guide
how I solve problems

01
Question first, design second
Before sketching a single screen, I focus on whether we're solving the right problem. Strong design starts with clarity, not creativity. A well-framed problem is already half the solution.
02
Always ask why
Behind every requirement is an assumption. Challenging it often reveals simpler, more effective solutions. The best ideas I've had started with someone saying "actually, why do we do it this way?"
03
The user is the compass
Business goals evolve, but user needs remain the anchor. I rely on research and real user insights to guide decisions — not trends, not opinions, and not what looks good in a presentation.
04
Impact over aesthetics
Design is successful when it creates change — in how people act, decide, or feel. Visuals matter, but outcomes matter more. Beautiful design that doesn't work is just decoration.
